## What This Application Is

The FITREP Evaluator is a **methodology demonstration tool** that walks you through a left-to-right marking framework designed to reduce grade inflation and confirmation bias. It:

- Provides **guidance only**
- Uses **synthetic test data exclusively**
- Runs **entirely in your browser** with no persistence or backend
- **Does not produce official output** suitable for chain-of-command submission

## What This Application Is NOT

The FITREP Evaluator is **NOT**:

- An authorized Fitness Report evaluation system
- A replacement for formal military training on MCO 1610.7B
- A substitute for the judgment and authority of the Reporting Senior
- Authorized to process, store, or retain real Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Connected to any Marine Corps database, personnel system, or official record
- Suitable for creating official evaluations for submission up any chain of command

## Methodology Limitations

The left-to-right marking methodology demonstrated in this Application:

1. **Is Unvalidated:** The methodology has not been formally tested, validated, or approved for official use in military personnel evaluation contexts.

2. **Applies Assumptions:** The methodology assumes trait descriptions, grade criteria, and the left-to-right progression are universally applicable. Your command may have different priorities, policies, or interpretation of standards.

3. **Does Not Replace Judgment:** The methodology is an aid to structure thinking, not an algorithmic replacement for professional judgment. The Reporting Senior remains responsible for all marking decisions.

4. **May Not Reduce Bias in Practice:** While the methodology is designed to address confirmation bias and grade inflation, individual application of the tool by humans may still introduce bias. Use of the tool does not guarantee objective, unbiased outcomes.

5. **Is Not Precedent:** Output from this Application is not precedent for any future evaluation. Each evaluation is independent.
